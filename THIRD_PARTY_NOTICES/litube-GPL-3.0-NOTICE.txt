Litube / MediaServiceCore playback adaptation

Upstream projects:
  https://github.com/HydeYYHH/litube (v4.0.0)
  https://github.com/HydeYYHH/MediaServiceCore
  (PlayerClientQueues.kt at 68f2f6a10dedf0842cbc99fa79d84bf1bfd9b21f)

Shiira's src/main/youtube-service.js and src/preload/youtube-player.js adapt
the playback approach in Litube's Extractor.kt, MscPlayback.kt,
VideoId.kt, PlaybackLoadErrorPolicy.kt and MediaServiceCore's
PlayerClientQueues.kt: a VisionOS-first direct-stream queue, shared extraction,
short-lived stream caching, stale-write guards, deferred/rejected clients and
bounded fresh-source recovery. The Kotlin/Media3 implementation has been
reworked for Electron/JavaScript, YouTube.js and Shaka Player. Android binaries
and the complete MediaServiceCore library are not bundled.
