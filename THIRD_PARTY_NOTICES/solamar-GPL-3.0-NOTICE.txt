Code adapted from Solamar in Shiira:

- src/main/arc-importer.js: Arc StorableSidebar.json parser, adapted from
  Solamar's src/main/arc-parse.ts and src/main/arc-import.ts.
- tests/arc-importer.test.js and tests/fixtures/arc-sidebar.json: adapted
  from Solamar's tests/arc-parse.test.ts and its fixture.
- src/main/extensions-service.js and src/renderer/modules/extensions.js:
  Chrome extension support, adapted from Solamar's src/main/extensions.ts
  and src/renderer/src/ExtensionsUI.tsx.

Features designed after Solamar and written for Shiira's renderer:
spaces, favorites, pinned tabs with folders, today tabs with automatic
archiving, tab sleep, the command bar, split view and Peek
(src/renderer/modules/sidebar.js, spaces-model.js, command-bar.js,
split-peek.js, src/main/spaces-service.js, src/renderer/sidebar.css).
